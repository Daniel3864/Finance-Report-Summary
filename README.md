# Finance-Report-Summary
An interactive Power BI financial analytics report evaluating $127.93M in Gross Sales, $118.73M in Net Sales, $16.89M in Profit, and 1.13M Units Sold from 2013 to 2014. Built to uncover revenue drivers, margin performance, and regional demand across six product lines, five customer segments, and key North American and European markets.

## Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard - Finance Report Summary](assets/dashboard-preview.png)

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Gross Sales:** $127.93M
* **Net Sales:** $118.73M
* **Total Profit:** $16.89M
* **Units Sold:** 1.13M

## Key Insights & Visual Breakdown
* **Product Performance:** *Paseo* leads total sales (~$33M+), followed by *VTT*, *Velo*, *Amarilla*, *Montana*, and *Carretera*.
* **Segment Distribution:** *Government* and *Small Business* generate the largest share of revenue, alongside *Enterprise*, *Midmarket*, and *Channel Partners*.
* **Profitability Trends:** Monthly profit tracking across 2013–2014 highlights seasonal peaks, reaching over $2.0M in late 2014.
* **Geographic Reach:** Tracks market demand across North America (*Canada*, *United States of America*, *Mexico*) and Europe (*France*, *Germany*).

## Dataset Schema
`Segment`, `Country`, `Product`, `Discount Band`, `Units Sold`, `Manufacturing Price`, `Sale Price`, `Gross Sales`, `Discounts`, `Sales`, `COGS`, `Profit`, `Date`, `Month Number`, `Month Name`, `Year`
